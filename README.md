## 🌟 Overview
Sponge Gallery Cleaner is a premium, beautifully designed Android application that helps you effortlessly clean your phone's gallery. It organizes your photos and videos by month and size, allowing you to quickly review, keep, or trash media items.

Engineered for extreme performance, Sponge can handle galleries with 50,000+ items with **zero startup lag** and **zero frame drops**.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Lightning Fast:** Instant startup, regardless of how many photos you have.
* **Month-by-Month Organization:** Automatically groups your media by month and year.
* **Smart Grid View:** View an entire month at a glance and bulk-select items to Trash or Keep instantly.
* **Large Files Detector:** Quickly find and eliminate massive videos (10MB to 1GB+).
* **Safe "Staging Bin":** Items you swipe to "Trash" are not deleted immediately. They go to a Staging Bin where you can review and restore them before permanently freeing up space.
* **Live Dashboard:** See your real-time phone storage usage and cleaning progress right on the Home Screen.
* **100% Private & Offline:** Sponge has no internet permission. Your photos never leave your device.

## 📱 User Manual (How to Use)

### 1. Home Screen Dashboard
When you open the app, you will see a Dashboard showing your phone's **Storage Usage** and the number of **Months Reviewed**.
Below that, your gallery is organized into clean, easy-to-tap categories.

### 2. Reviewing a Month
Tap on any Month (e.g., *September 2026*) to start cleaning:
* **Swipe Right** to Keep the photo.
* **Swipe Left** to send it to the Trash.
* **Tap the Top Strip** to quickly rewind or skip to a specific photo.

### 3. Bulk Cleaning (Grid View)
If you have too many photos in a month, tap the **Grid Icon** in the top-right corner.
* Tap to select multiple photos at once.
* Hit the red **Trash (X)** button at the bottom to bulk-delete them, or the green **Keep (X)** button to save them.

### 4. Freeing Up Space
Once you've trashed some items, return to the Home Screen and tap **"Ready to free up X MB"**.
* This opens the Staging Bin.
* **Made a mistake?** Tap the "Restore" icon on any photo to put it back in your gallery.
* **Ready?** Tap "Permanently Delete" at the bottom to securely remove the items and reclaim your storage space.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security
Sponge is built with a bank-grade security mindset:
* **No Internet Access:** The app cannot connect to the internet. Period.
* **OS-Level Deletion:** Sponge delegates actual file deletion to the Android Operating System, meaning it cannot secretly delete system files.
* **Zero Tracking:** No analytics, no crashlytics, no hidden background trackers.
